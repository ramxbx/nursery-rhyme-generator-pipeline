A big brown bear with sleepy eyes,
Woke up beneath the birch tree skies.
He stretched his paws, he scratched his back,
And ambled down the woodland track.

He found a stream of icy blue,
Where silver fish went darting through.
He dipped a paw, he missed them all,
And tumbled in with quite a fall.

He shook the water from his fur,
And made the fallen pine cones stir.
A squirrel laughed from up above,
And dropped an acorn like a glove.

The berries hung in purple rows,
He ate them up on tippy-toes.
Then home he went with belly round,
And slept the winter safe and sound.
