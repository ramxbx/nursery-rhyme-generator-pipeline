A honeybee in yellow stripes,
Flew past the garden's climbing pipes.
She hummed a tune from flower to flower,
And gathered gold for half an hour.

The roses opened wide and red,
The daisies nodded every head.
The lavender stood tall and blue,
And offered her a sip of dew.

She dusted both her fuzzy knees,
With pollen from the apple trees.
Her basket filled, her wings grew tired,
The golden light was all she required.

Then home she flew to hive and comb,
Where all the busy bees go home.
They danced to say where flowers grow,
And made the sweetest honey glow.
