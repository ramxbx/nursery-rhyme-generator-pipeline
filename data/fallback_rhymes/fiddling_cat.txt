A ginger cat with whiskers wide,
Sat down upon the riverside.
He tuned a fiddle made of pine,
And played a tune at half past nine.

The frogs came hopping two by two,
The heron danced, the swallows flew.
A field mouse tapped his tiny feet,
Beside a patch of meadowsweet.

The music floated down the stream,
As pretty as a summer dream.
The willow branches swayed along,
And every reed knew every song.

And when the final note was played,
The dancers bowed in the evening shade.
He tucked his fiddle in its case,
And washed the moonlight off his face.
