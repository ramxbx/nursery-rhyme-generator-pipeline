A jolly snowman round and tall,
Stood proudly by the garden wall.
Two lumps of coal became his eyes,
A carrot nose of perfect size.

The children wrapped a woolly scarf,
And drew a smile to make him laugh.
They gave him twiggy arms to hold,
Against the morning bright and cold.

The robins came to share his crumbs,
The church bells rang their frosty drums.
The snow lay deep on every roof,
And every step became a hoof.

He watched the sleds go whizzing by,
Beneath a pale and silver sky.
And though the sun made him grow small,
He was the finest of them all.
