A little yellow duck named Sue,
Went walking where the tall reeds grew.
She dipped her beak into the pond,
And watched the ripples spread beyond.

The water sparkled clear and bright,
The morning sun was warm and light.
She paddled past a lily flower,
And splashed about for half an hour.

A dragonfly flew swooping low,
And landed on her back to go.
They drifted underneath the trees,
And rocked upon the gentle breeze.

When evening painted skies with gold,
And little ducks grow sleepy-cold,
She tucked her beak beneath her wing,
And dreamed of every splashy thing.
