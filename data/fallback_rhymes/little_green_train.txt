A little train of bright green paint,
Chugged up a hill with no complaint.
Her wheels went round, her whistle blew,
She carried apples, corn, and dew.

The hill was steep, the hill was long,
She sang herself a climbing song.
"I think I can, I think I may,"
She puffed along the winding way.

The cows looked up from clover fields,
The farmer waved from wooden wheels.
A flock of geese flew overhead,
And honked above the track ahead.

And at the top she stopped to rest,
The valley shining east and west.
Then down she rolled with happy cheer,
The finest little train this year.
