A little red hen with speckled wings,
Went scratching where the barnyard sings.
She found a seed beside the shed,
A tiny seed of golden bread.

"Who'll help me plant this seed?" she said.
The lazy goose just shook his head.
The sleepy pig began to snore,
The cat curled up beside the door.

So she alone dug up the ground,
And patted all the soft earth round.
She watered it at break of day,
And chased the hungry crows away.

The wheat grew tall, the wheat grew gold,
The finest loaf was baked and sold.
And when it steamed upon her plate,
Three hungry friends stood at the gate.
