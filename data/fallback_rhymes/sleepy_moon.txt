The moon came out at half past eight,
A silver smile above the gate.
She peeked between the chimney tops,
And past the sleepy flower shops.

She shone upon the garden wall,
She shone upon the poplars tall,
She laid a stripe across the floor,
And slipped beneath the bedroom door.

The owl looked up and blinked his eye,
The old clock chimed a soft reply.
The village hushed, the lamps went low,
The rooftops wore a coat of snow.

So close your eyes and do not peep,
The moon is guarding all your sleep.
She'll keep her lantern burning bright,
Until the morning takes the night.
