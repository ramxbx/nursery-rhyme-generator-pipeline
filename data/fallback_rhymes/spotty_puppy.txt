A spotty puppy small and white,
Lost his red ball on Sunday night.
He searched beneath the kitchen chair,
He searched the stairs, it wasn't there.

He sniffed along the garden path,
He looked inside the birdie bath.
He dug a hole beside the tree,
But found a stone instead of three.

He asked the cat who would not say,
He asked the hens who ran away.
He sat down sadly by the gate,
And thought that he would have to wait.

But then he heard a gentle sound,
The ball came rolling down the mound.
A breeze had pushed it from the shed,
And bumped it gently on his head.
